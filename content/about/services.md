## Research Software Quality Kit

{{< figure
  src="images/services/RSQKitPreview-thumb.png"
  alt="Preview of RSQKit"
  class="float-right m-2 w-[50%]"
  href="https://everse.software/RSQKit/"

The [**Research Software Quality Kit (RSQKit)**](https://everse.software/RSQKit/) is a community-curated knowledge base that brings together best practices, quality assessment guidance and expertise to support high-quality research software across different disciplines. It connects guidance, tools, research software stories and training resources, helping researchers who code and Research Software Engineers (RSEs) improve software quality and sustainability. 

<font size="+2"> Find out **[more *about* RSQKit](/services/rsqkit/)** </font>

## EVERSE Technology Radar

{{< figure
  src="images/services/TechRadarPreview-thumb.png"
  alt="Preview of EVERSE TechRadar"
  class="float-right m-2 w-[50%]"
  href="https://everse.software/TechRadar/"
  target="_self"
  nozoom="true"
>}}
The [**EVERSE Technology Radar**](https://everse.software/TechRadar/) collects, classifies and presents tools and services that help assess and enhance research software quality. It combines a curated catalogue with a visual dashboard, organising tools according to the EVERSE Quality Dimensions. Complementing the guidance and best practices in RSQKit, TechRadar helps researchers identify tools and services to improve their software.

<font size="+2"> Find out **[more *about* TechRadar](/services/techradar/)** </font>

## DashVERSE

{{< figure
  src="images/services/DashVersePreview-thumb.png"
  alt="Preview of DashVERSE"

  href="https://www.dashverse.cloud/"

[**DashVERSE**](https://www.dashverse.cloud/) is a dashboard prototype developed for the EVERSE project. It brings together research software quality assessments, metrics and indicators across scientific clusters and infrastructures, providing actionable insights through role-based dashboards for policy makers, principal investigators, Research Software Engineers, researchers who code and trainers.

## EVERSE Training
{{< figure
  src="images/services/EVERSETrainingPreview-thumb.png"
  alt="Preview of EVERSE Training"
  class="float-right m-2 w-[50%]"
  href="https://everse-training.app.cern.ch/"
  target="_self"
  nozoom="true"
>}}

[**EVERSE Training**](https://everse-training.app.cern.ch/) is a community-curated training catalogue for early career scientists and engineers learning best practices in research software engineering. It provides a one-stop shop where trainees can discover and filter training materials, and trainers can share courses, exercises, podcasts, slides and tutorials. Drawing on contributions from across research institutions, the catalogue links to materials rather than hosting them directly.

<font size="+2"> Find out **[more *about* EVERSE Training](/services/eversetraining/)** </font>
