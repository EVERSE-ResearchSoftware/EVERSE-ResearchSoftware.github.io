Selim Kalayci is a Senior Science Officer at the [ELIXIR Hub](https://elixir-europe.org/), where he works on European collaborative projects and initiatives supporting research software, scientific computing and research infrastructures. He has a background in computer science, with a PhD from Florida International University (USA). Before joining ELIXIR, he worked in academic and research environments in the United States and Türkiye, focusing on distributed computing, scientific workflows, bioinformatics and the development of computational tools for biomedical research.

His experience spans research, software development, teaching and the coordination of international collaborations. Within EVERSE, Selim contributes primarily to [WP1](/workpackages/01_framework_european_network/), which focuses on community engagement, collaboration and capacity building across the European research software ecosystem.
