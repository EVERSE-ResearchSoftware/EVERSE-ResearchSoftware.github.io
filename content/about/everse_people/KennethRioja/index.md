Kenneth Rioja is involved in [Work Package 5](/workpackages/05_capacity_and_recognition/) (capacity building and recognition) as the developer and maintainer of the [EVERSE Training Catalogue](/services/eversetraining) which centralizes training resources made by the RSE community about best practices for Research Software Engineering. To know more about it, [watch the webinar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=njAwqtLFpC8) about EVERSE Training Catalogue.

Kenneth holds a [Master in Learning and Teaching Technologies](https://maltt.unige.ch/) from the [University of Geneva](https://unige.ch), his [master's thesis](http://dx.doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.36751.19365/1) focused on explorating and clustering players' strategies in an educational video game played in a museum. He was also a Research Assistant and Lab Tech at the [Brain And Learning Lab](https://www.unige.ch/fapse/brainlearning/) which focused on the impact of video games and technology use (e.g., [multitasking](https://doi.org/10.1037/tmb0000106)) on brain plasticity and attention.
