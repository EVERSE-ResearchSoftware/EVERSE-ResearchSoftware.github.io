The [**EVERSE Technology Radar**](https://everse.software/TechRadar/) is part of the EVERSE project and serves as a platform to collect, classify, and present tools and services that help assess and enhance Research Software Quality.

{{< flex-content >}}
<--->
{{< figure src="techradar.png">}}
{{< /flex-content>}}

It consists of two main elements:

{{< flex-content >}}
{{< figure src="tools-icon.png" >}}

**A catalogue of tools and services**
designed to evaluate, measure, and improve the quality of research software.
<--->
{{< figure src="radar-icon.png" >}}

**The TechRadar**
a visual dashboard that displays this catalogue in an accessible and intuitive way.
{{< /flex-content >}}


**How is the TechRadar linked to other EVERSE outputs?**
